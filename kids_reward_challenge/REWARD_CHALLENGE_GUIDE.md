# HPC&A 2D Reward & Activity Challenge Suite
### Science Souvenir Distribution System for Kids, Teens & Students
**Designed by Nima** | Commissioned for the **HPC&A Research Group** (High Performance Computing & Architecture)

---

## 🎯 Overview & Concept

This suite turns the 3D-printed souvenirs into tangible, educational rewards for workshop participants, science fairs, school visits, and open house events:

1. **Junior Edition (Kids / Primary School):**
   - **Model:** **Chibi Kawaii Anime GPU** (`gpu_fantasy_chibi`)
   - **Blocks:** **6 Blocks** ($3 \times 2$ Grid)
   - **Focus:** Cheerful visual assembly, motor skills (scissors & glue), basic computing concepts (fans, cooling, supercomputing brain).

2. **Advanced Edition (Teens / Secondary School & Students):**
   - **Model:** **Superconducting Transmon QPU Chip** (`qpu_keychain_hpca`)
   - **Blocks:** **12 Blocks** ($4 \times 3$ Grid — *Increased difficulty!*)
   - **Focus:** Real quantum hardware architecture (microwave coplanar waveguides, transmon crosses, 15 mK dilution cooling, 28 gold wirebond contact pads).

## 🖨️ Printing & Workshop Instructions

1. **Paper Standard:** Standard ISO A4 ($210 \times 297\text{ mm}$).
2. **Printer Settings:**
   - Orientation: **Portrait** (Vertical).
   - Scaling: **100% (Do not fit/shrink)** to preserve true proportions.
   - Quality: Color print (Normal or High).
3. **Materials Needed for Participants:**
   - Children's safety scissors (punta redonda).
   - Glue sticks (barra de pegamento).
   - Colored pencils / markers (for coloring sheets).
   - Stamp / marker for booth instructors.
4. **Prize Redemption Flow:**
   - Participant completes the 6 (or 12) blocks.
   - Participant brings the board/passport to the HPC&A booth.
   - Instructor checks the pieces / answers and signs/stamps the certificate.
   - Participant receives the real **3D-printed PLA Keychain**!
